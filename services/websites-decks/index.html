Where I come in

You know how to explain it in a room. The story needs to work when you’re not there.

Your strongest point comes halfway through the deck. The website explains every feature, but leaves people wondering why it matters to them. You keep filling in the gaps on calls because the materials aren’t doing enough of the work.

What I look for

Where people lose the thread.

  • The value comes too late

    People work through introductions, features, and background before finding out why they should care.

  • One story for every audience

    Investors, buyers, and users arrive with different questions. The same message leaves some of them unanswered.

  • Everything gets equal weight

    There’s plenty to say, but no clear order. The point people need to remember competes with everything else.

  • Claims without enough proof

    The promises are there. The examples, results, and details that make them believable are harder to find.

  • A different story in every place

    Your website, pitch, and sales deck explain the business differently. Each needs its own emphasis, with a shared story underneath.

  • An unclear next step

    People understand what you do, but don’t have enough reason to enquire, try the product, or continue the conversation.

How I think about the work

Start with the impact we want to create.

A buyer deciding whether to book a call needs different information from an investor weighing an opportunity. I work backwards from those decisions to shape what we say, what we show, and how the experience unfolds.

  1. 01

    Give each audience a way in

    I get clear on what matters to each group, what they need to understand, and what might hold them back. That guides the message and the route through the content.

  2. 02

    Let the narrative lead

    I find the connection between what you do and why someone should care. That thread shapes the structure, the proof, and the design.

  3. 03

    Bring the right people into the work

    I lead the narrative and design, develop the frontend, and bring in copywriting and development specialists where needed. I stay close to the work through reviews and delivery to make sure the quality holds throughout.

  4. 04

    Put proof where it matters

    A result, a product example, or a customer story can answer a question at the right moment. I build that evidence into the story.

  5. 05

    Carry the thinking through to delivery

    The details need to hold up in the finished website or presentation. I review how it reads, looks, and works in the situations people will actually encounter it.
